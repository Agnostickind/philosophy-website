## Branches of Philosophy

- [Ethics](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/ethics-branch-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of morality, values, virtue, and right action.

- [Metaphysics](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/metaphysics-branch-philosophy.html): The study of reality, existence, being, identity, causation, and possibility.

- [Epistemology](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/epistemology-branch-philosophy.html): The study of knowledge, belief, justification, truth, and evidence.

- [Logic](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/logic-branch-philosophy.html): The study of reasoning, inference, arguments, and validity.

- [Aesthetics](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/aesthetics-branch-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of beauty, art, taste, and aesthetic experience.

- [Philosophy of Mind](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/mind-philosophy.html): The study of consciousness, mental states, perception, thought, identity, and the relationship between mind and body.

- [Philosophy of Language](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/language-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of meaning, reference, truth, communication, interpretation, and the relationship between language and thought.

- [Political Philosophy](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/political-philosophy.html): The study of political authority, justice, liberty, rights, equality, government, and the organization of society.

- [Philosophy of Science](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/science-philosophy.html): The study of scientific knowledge, explanation, evidence, methods, theories, and the nature and limits of scientific inquiry.

- [Philosophy of Religion](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/religion-philosophy.html): The philosophical examination of religious belief, God, faith, reason, religious experience, and questions concerning existence and meaning.

- [Philosophy of Education](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/education-philosophy.html): The study of the aims, nature, methods, values, and purposes of education.

- [Philosophy of History](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/history-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of historical knowledge, interpretation, historical change, causation, and the meaning or direction of history.

- [Philosophy of Law](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/law-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of law, legal authority, justice, rights, obligation, punishment, and the relationship between law and morality.

- [Philosophy of Mathematics](https://agnostickind.github.io/philosophy-website/maths-philosophy.html): The philosophical study of mathematical objects, truth, proof, foundations, abstraction, and the nature of mathematical knowledge.
